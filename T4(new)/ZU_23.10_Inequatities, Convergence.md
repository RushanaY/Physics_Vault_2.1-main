# Inequlities
## Markov inequality 
$$P(X > x) \leq \frac{E(X)}{X} \quad , \quad x>0$$
Example: a photon source is producing a number of photons and we want to get the probability of the source producing above a sertain amount of photons. 

### Proof
$$\begin{align}
E(X) &= \int_0^{\infty} x' f(x') dx' \\
&= \int_0^x x' f(x') dx' + \int_x^{\infty} x' f(x') dx' \\
& \geq x \int_0^{\infty} f(x') dx' \\
\end{align}$$
=> $$E(X) \leq x P(X>x)$$
## Tschebyscheff inequality 
$$P(|X-E(X)|\geq \epsilon) \leq \frac{V(X)}{\epsilon^2}$$

## Jense's inequality 
for G konvex: $$\langle G(X) \rangle \geq G(\langle x \rangle )$$
for G konkav: $$\langle G(X) \rangle \leq G(\langle x \rangle )$$
