two wires -> probe current => resistance is unimportant because we know the current 
two wires -> measures voltage drop => has very high impedance and therefore there is no current flow and no resistance (resistance impossible to know) 

$$R_0 = \frac{\rho l}{A}$$ $l$ - length of wore
$A$ - area
$\rho$ - density at $T_{ref}$ 

$$R(T) = R_0 + R_0 \alpha_{\rho} (T- T_{ref})$$
$\alpha_{\rho}$ - temperature coefficient for resistivity (material specific)
