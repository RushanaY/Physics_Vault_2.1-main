# Poisson equation
$$\vec \triangledown^2 \phi = - \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$
with the [[Laplace Operator]]
# Derivation 
Conditions: $$\vec J =0, \qquad \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} =0$$
last step being the only non trivial [[Maxwell equations|Maxwell equation]] $$\vec \triangledown \cdot \vec E =- \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$
*here we look just at the [[electrostatics]]  case. Putting the flux, density time derivative to zero and putting them into the maxwell equations, we get only one equation that is not equal to zero (is non trivial). But from the first step, we have the $\vec E = - \triangledown \phi$, which we put into $\vec \triangledown \cdot \vec E$ and get the Poisson equation $\vec \triangledown^2 \phi$ *
