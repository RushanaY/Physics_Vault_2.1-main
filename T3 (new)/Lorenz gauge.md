$$\frac{1}{c^2} \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \phi + \vec \triangledown \cdot \vec A \overset{!} = 0$$
or in shorter notation $$\partial_{\mu} A^{\mu} =0$$
these gives the basic equations of electrodynamic: 
$$\square \phi = - \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$
$$\square \vec A = - \mu_0 \vec J$$
In addition of relativity: 
$$\partial_{\mu} \partial^{\mu} A^{\nu} = - \mu_0 J^{\nu} \qquad , \qquad \partial_{\mu} A^{\mu} =0$$
