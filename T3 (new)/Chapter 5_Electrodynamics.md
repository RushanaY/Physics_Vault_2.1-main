# 5.1. Equations of electrodynamics 
fundemental field = gauge field $$A^{\mu} (x^{\mu})$$
defines the gauge transformations $$(1)\quad \phi \to \phi' = \phi - \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \chi \quad, \quad \vec A \to \vec A ' = \vec A' + \vec \triangledown \chi$$
which gives the gauge invariant $E$ and $B$ fields $$(2)\quad \vec E = - \vec \triangledown \phi - \frac{\partial }{\partial t} \vec A \quad, \quad \vec B = \vec \triangledown \times \vec A$$
which together is $$(3) \quad \vec \triangledown \times \vec E + \frac{\partial }{\partial t} \vec B =0\quad, \quad \vec \triangledown \cdot \vec B =0$$
