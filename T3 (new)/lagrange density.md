with $$\partial_{\mu} \phi = \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial x^{\mu}}, \qquad x^{\mu} = (ct, \vec x)$$


$$\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{2} (\epsilon_0|\vec E|^2 - \frac{1}{\mu_0}|\vec B|^2) - \phi \rho + \vec A \cdot \vec J$$

in [[lorentz invariant]] style 
$$ \mathcal{L} = - \frac{1}{4 \mu_0} F^{\mu \nu} F_{\mu \nu} + A_{\mu} J^{\mu}$$
