# 4.1 Light atom interaction operator 
$$V = - \vec d \cdot \vec E$$here:
- $\vec d$ is the [[dipol moment]] 
- $\vec E$ is the [[electric field]] 

this describes the energy of a particle, that is being influenced by an external (oscillating) electric field
